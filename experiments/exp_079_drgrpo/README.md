## Takeaways

1. **The per-token shaping is additive across token-level RL algorithms.** On the learnable
   datasets it lifts GRPO, DAPO, and Dr.GRPO by large margins (L50 Δ up to +0.89), and every
   peak-table shows Ours reaching the baseline's *final* peak in **~⅓–½ the steps** — a
   convergence-speed story as strong as the accuracy one.
2. **Dr.GRPO + shaping is the top learnable-set configuration** (peaks +2.67 / +2.51 / +2.36),
   consistent with our length-bias analysis: removing Dr.GRPO's length/difficulty bias and
   adding our decisive-token credit compound.
3. **GSPO is the boundary case.** Its tight *sequence-level* importance clipping partly
   absorbs the per-token advantage structure: shaping still accelerates early convergence
   (reaches GSPO's peak 4–7× faster on MATH-500/BigMath) but does not improve final accuracy.
   This delimits *how* the method works — through per-token credit — an honest scope
   statement, not a failure.
4. **Baselines alone under-perform plain GRPO here** (DAPO/GSPO/Dr.GRPO all ≤ GRPO at 300
   steps, ng=4) — the shaping is what makes them competitive/best.
5. **Hard set (Omni-MATH) unchanged within noise** for every method — the open problem, and
   the motivation for the exp_071–075 principled roadmap (gate / bounded signal / budget).
