To determine the percentage of the total future minimum sponsorship and other marketing payments that will be due in the next 12-month period, we need to find out the amount due in the next 12 months and the total amount of future minimum obligations.

```python
# Get the earliest future period label
earliest_period = get_earliest_future_period_label()

# Get the obligation amount for the earliest period
obligation_earliest_period = get_obligation_amount_for_period(earliest_period, obligation_type="minimum")

# Get the total obligation amount
total_obligation_amount = get_total_obligation_amount(obligation_type="minimum")

# Calculate the percentage
percentage = (obligation_earliest_period / total_obligation_amount) * 100

# Round the percentage to one decimal place
rounded_percentage = round(percentage, 1)

print(f"{rounded_percentage}%")
```
