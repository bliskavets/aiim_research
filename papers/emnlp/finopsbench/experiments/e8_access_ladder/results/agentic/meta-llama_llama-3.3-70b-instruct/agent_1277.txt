We use the get_reports_for_company tool with the company id obtained in step 1 to get a list of reports issued by the company.

## Step 3: Filter the reports to get the report ids for 2006 and 2007
From the list of reports obtained in step 2, we filter to get the report ids for the fiscal years 2006 and 2007.

## Step 4: List the depreciation categories for the reports
We use the list_categories tool with the report ids obtained in step 3 to list all the depreciation categories for the 2006 and 2007 reports.

## Step 5: Get the amount for the deductions category
We use the get_depreciation_amount tool with the report ids and the category "depreciation and amortization – deductions (dispositions and retirements of property)" to get the amounts for 2006 and 2007.

## Step 6: Calculate the increase in deductions
We use the compute_difference tool with the amounts for 2007 and 2006 to calculate the increase in deductions.

## Step 7: Calculate the difference
