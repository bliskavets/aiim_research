The total outstanding number of shares that received dividends in 2016 can be calculated by dividing the total dividends paid by the dividends per share. Total dividends paid in 2016 were $2.0 billion, and the dividend per share was $6.77. Thus, the number of shares = 2,000,000,000 / 6.77 ≈ 295.43 million shares.
