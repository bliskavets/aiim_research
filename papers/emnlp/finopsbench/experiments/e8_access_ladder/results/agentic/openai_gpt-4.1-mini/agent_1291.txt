The average uncompounded annual return for JPMorgan Chase over the five-year period from December 31, 2013 to December 31, 2018 is calculated as follows:

Initial value (2013): 100.0
Final value (2018): 203.07

Total return over 5 years = 203.07 - 100.0 = 103.07
Average annual return = (103.07 / 5) = 20.6%

Answer: 20.6%
